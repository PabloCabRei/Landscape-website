01

Riverside Revitalization Project

What began as a neglected industrial waterfront became the city's most beloved public space. We transformed 8 kilometers of riverbank into a continuous greenway featuring bioswales for stormwater management, native riparian plantings, and community gathering spaces.

Location Portland, Oregon
Duration 18 Months
Area 32 Hectares

02

The Vertical Forest Tower

An ambitious mixed-use development where landscape architecture meets vertical urbanism. We designed integrated planting systems across 40 floors, creating a living building that improves air quality, reduces energy costs, and provides habitat for urban wildlife.

Location Singapore
Duration 3 Years
Plants 15,000+ Species

03

Desert Oasis Resort

In one of the world's driest regions, we created a luxurious resort landscape that uses 70% less water than conventional designs. Through xeriscaping, greywater recycling, and innovative shade structures, we proved sustainability and luxury can coexist beautifully.

Location Dubai, UAE
Duration 24 Months
Water Saved 2.4M Liters/Year

About Us

Get in touch with our team of landscape architects, horticulturists, and ecological designers.

Designing Nature's Future

Founded in 2008, Verdant Studio is an award-winning landscape architecture firm dedicated to creating outdoor spaces that honor both human experience and ecological integrity. Our multidisciplinary team brings together expertise in botany, ecology, engineering, and fine arts to deliver landscapes that are as functional as they are beautiful.
